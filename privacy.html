Privacy notice

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Who we are

This site is run by DDbyAlfonzo, a design and development studio in Johannesburg, South Africa. For anything about your personal information, contact us through the form on this site.

What we collect

When you send the contact form, we receive your name, email address, the type of project you chose and your message. We use these only to reply to you and to carry out the project we discuss.

How your message reaches us

The form is delivered by Formspree, a form-handling service that passes your message to our email inbox. Formspree processes it on our behalf and stores it under its own privacy policy.

Analytics and cookies

This site uses no analytics, tracking pixels or cookies. Your visit is not recorded or profiled.

Your rights

Under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), you can ask what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, and object to how it is used. Send us a message and we will respond. You may also lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa.

Keeping it safe

We keep your information only as long as needed for our conversation or project, and we never sell it.
