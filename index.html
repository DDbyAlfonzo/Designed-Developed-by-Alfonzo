How a project runs

  1. 01

    Sketch

    A call to understand your business, then a brief and rough sketches of the plan.

    2–3 days
  2. 02

    Design

    Brand and screens designed in detail, with two rounds of feedback built in.

    1–2 weeks
  3. 03

    Build

    Developed, tested on phones and laptops, and reviewed with you before launch.

    2–4 weeks
  4. 04

    Launch

    Domain, hosting and email set up, the site goes live, and you get a handover.

    2–3 days

Typical timings for a business website. Smaller jobs like a logo or poster take days, not weeks.
