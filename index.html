FlowLo

One app for your stock, orders and deliveries. Clean stock, clean orders, faster sales.

FlowLo is an operations app for small businesses that sell from a kitchen counter, a café till or WhatsApp. It keeps every order moving from the shelf to the doorstep, so owners spend less time on admin and more time selling.

  • Inventory tracker with low-stock alerts before you run out
  • Incoming orders, including WhatsApp orders, in one list
  • Delivery board that follows each order from confirmed to delivered and paid
  • Daily takings and a dashboard that flags what needs attention today
  • Customer updates so buyers know where their order is

Designed and built end to end by Alfonzo · Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind, Firebase, Framer Motion

Questions

Your questions answered

How do we start a project?

Send a message through the form below. I'll reply within one working day to set up a short call, then send a brief and a quote based on what you need.

How long does a website take?

A typical business website takes from the first call to launch. Smaller jobs, like a logo or a poster, take days rather than weeks.

Do you design and build, or only design?

Both. I design the screens and build them in code, so you deal with one person from the first sketch to the live site.

Can you redesign or audit my existing site or app?

Yes. I can run a UX audit that lists what's getting in your customers' way and what to fix first, then help with the redesign.

What do you need from me to start?

Your goals, who your customers are, any brand assets you already have (logo, colours, photos) and examples of sites you like.

Do you work with clients outside South Africa?

Yes. I'm based in Johannesburg and work remotely with clients anywhere, using video calls and shared Figma files.
